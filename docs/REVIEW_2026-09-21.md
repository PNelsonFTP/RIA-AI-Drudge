Documentation-only review of the local tree at
`/Users/paulnelson/Documents/Development/RIA-AI-Drudge/`. No application code,
sources, or workflows were changed. Evidence is the code plus the checked-in
payload `public/data/headlines.json` and `public/data/brief.json` generated

This review does not recommend creating the public GitHub repo or Pages.

## What's solid

- Third-party fetch stays at build time. The browser reads static JSON only.
- UK AISI and Gray Swan are gone. `scripts/scrape-sources.ts` scrapes Anthropic
  News and Anthropic Research only. They are not in `SOURCES`.
- The feed-health hard fail is already **0.75**, with a warn at 0.85 and a
  30-day max displayed age (`scripts/check-data.ts`). A 0.40 hard fail is not
  what this tree runs. The 2026-09-20 payload would pass 75% and 85%.
- Short-token `requireAny` does what the tests claim: a title can start with
  “AI”, and “available” / “said” / “section” do not match `ai` or `sec`
  (`scripts/lib/requireAny.ts`, `scripts/lib/requireAny.test.ts`).
- Site lead rank, the vendor cage for Shopify / Dreamforce / PtEverywhere, and
  press-over-wire inside a cluster are implemented and covered by
  `scripts/lib/router.test.ts` and `scripts/lib/groupStories.test.ts`.
- `scripts/types.ts` and `src/lib/types.ts` match today, including `vendor?`.
  Sharing them is already P3.16 in `docs/FUTURE_IMPROVEMENTS.md`.
- Vite `base` is `/RIA-AI-Drudge/` (`vite.config.ts`). The three-column wire
  follows `CATEGORIES` index modulo 3 (`src/App.tsx`).

## Bugs / risks

### 1. Unfiltered Google News queries put non-AI stories on the homepage

`scripts/sources.ts` leaves these feeds without `requireAny`:

- `Reuters AI (GN)` and `Axios AI (GN)` (industry)
- `GN: wealthtech AI` (wealthtech)
- Finextra AI, American Banker AI, and the other “already scoped” channel feeds

Google’s boolean query is not a filter. On the 2026-09-20 payload, Industry
rows 9, 11, and 12 are Reuters wires that **fail** `AI_FILTER`: global shares /
inflation, the FedEx–InPost takeover, and Beretta Holding’s offer for Sturm
Ruger. Axios items in the View All tail are the same class (credit tightening,
Buffett, a Premier League holding-company sale). None of those summaries
contain an AI token; they are title plus the publisher name.

Wealthtech row 1 is `GN: wealthtech AI`: “UPI MDR could upend lending,
wealth-tech economics.” It also fails `AI_FILTER`. It is the column lead, so
`scripts/lib/emitFeed.ts` puts it in the site Atom feed, and
`scripts/generate-brief.ts` copies it into the brief (bullet 5 of
`public/data/brief.json`).

Finextra’s AI channel is not AI-only. “Mind the gap: What’s missing from modern
resilience?” is banking row 2 and fails `AI_FILTER`.

`AI_FILTER` on the Reuters, Axios, and wealthtech GN entries would drop these
items. Their stored summaries do not echo the search query.

### 2. Dedup deletes the second source before trending can see it

`scripts/fetch-feeds.ts` drops a later article when the normalized URL **or the
lowercased title** was already seen, then sorts by priority. The winner is
whoever appears first in `SOURCES`, which is not the higher-priority or
non-aggregator source.

A publisher item and a Google News twin of the same headline collapse to one
row. The losing source name never reaches `scripts/lib/router.ts`, so
`pickTrendingLead` and `groupStories` cannot prefer trade press, and the story
cannot become a 2-source trend.

Stories that trend are the ones with **different** titles. On this payload that
is three clusters, all with `sourceCount` 2: an on-topic Wealth Solutions
Report / Anthropic item, then an FT “AI Force” markets story, then a Verge
Gemini item. General AI outlets cluster; RIA coverage that shares a headline
does not. `docs/FUTURE_IMPROVEMENTS.md` P1.4 (warn under 4, maybe relax 72h
backfill, do not lower Jaccard) does not describe this.

### 3. The quality gate cannot see a topical failure, and a total outage skips it

`scripts/check-data.ts` hard-fails on feed OK ratio, max age, an empty category
list, `totalCount === 0`, and more than half of sections empty. It does not
look at titles. The 2026-09-20 build is 91.7% OK with the Reuters and UPI
rows above, so `build:check` exits 0. Fourteen OK feeds contributed 0 items
and still count toward the ratio (AdvisorHub, most bank regulators, MarketWatch,
Orion, and others). That warn is non-fatal.

`scripts/build-data.ts` exits **0** when fetch plus scrape return zero
articles and a previous `headlines.json` exists. `.github/workflows/refresh.yml`
then runs `build:check` on yesterday’s good file, commits nothing, and still
deploys. A total fetch outage stays green.

Undated items are a latent hole. `ageHours` in `scripts/lib/score.ts` treats a
missing date as exactly 21 days, and the router keeps an item when age is
**greater than** `hardDays` (regulation, compliance, and research use 21). An
undated item is kept. `check-data.ts` skips it when computing max age.
`src/lib/timeAgo.ts` uses a different rule: missing dates are infinite for
`ageHours`, and `isStale` returns false, so the row is not dimmed.

### 4. The brief trusts column order, and Claude can publish uncited copy

`scripts/generate-brief.ts` fallback:

- `hasRiaItem` is regulation, advisor tech, wealthtech, and practice.
  Compliance is a home category in `RIA_HOME_CATEGORIES` and is omitted here.
  On a day when only compliance has RIA copy, an industry / labs / markets
  trending lead still becomes the headline.
- Variety sampling takes `articles[0]` from each category. That is how the UPI
  story and the Abnormal Returns linkdump (“Sunday links: the AI safety
  debate”) entered `brief.json`. The linkdump is already P2.12. The UPI path
  is not.
- The Claude path keeps `headline` and `bullets` when every cited URL fails
  the allow-list. `citedArticles` can be empty and `source` is still
  `"claude"`. There is no test for this function.

### 5. `requireAny` still misses real tokens, and long needles are substrings

Confirmed against `matchesRequireAny`:

| Text | Needle | Result |
|------|--------|--------|
| “RIAs adopt tools” | `ria` | no match (`\bria\b` loses to the plural) |
| “A.I. supervision update” | `ai` | no match |
| “Firms roll out GPT4” | `gpt` | no match (hyphen and “chatgpt” are special-cased; a glued version is not) |
| “xAI raises a round” | `ai` | no match |
| “cybersecurity budget” | `security` | match (`FINANCE_OR_ENTERPRISE`) |
| “deregulation push” | `regulation` | match |

Word boundaries apply only at length ≤ 3. `KEYWORDS` in `scripts/sources.ts`
has the same class of gap on the routing side: `"ria "` and `"sec "` require a
following space, so “RIAs” and “SEC’s” do not route.

A match anywhere in the 320-character summary is enough. Bloomberg Markets
“Marsh CEO Says Geopolitics Tops CEO Risks” leads AI MARKETS because the
summary mentions AI near the end of the blurb.

### 6. Aggregator detection does not know the real Google News source names

`isAggregatorSource` in `scripts/lib/router.ts` treats `GN:` prefixes and the
substring `Google News` as aggregators. The configured names are
`Reuters AI (GN)` and `Axios AI (GN)`. Both return false. `isWeakClusterLead`
in `scripts/lib/groupStories.ts` only checks `startsWith("GN:")`.

`scripts/lib/router.test.ts` asserts `isAggregatorSource("Reuters AI (Google News)")`,
which is not a name in `SOURCES`. A Reuters wire inside a cluster can win the
trending or in-column lead.

### 7. Muted-lead fallback order disagrees with the build-time rank

`RIA_LEAD_RANK` in `scripts/lib/router.ts` is regulation, advisor tech,
wealthtech, **compliance**, practice. The client fallback in `src/App.tsx`
(about line 242) is regulation, advisor tech, wealthtech, **practice**,
compliance, industry. The brief’s `leadCatOrder` matches the client, not the
rank. When `leadUrl` is missing or its source is muted, a practice linkdump
outranks a compliance story.

Related lead quality, same payload: the site lead is SEC Speeches, “Remarks
Before 12th Annual Government Enforcement Institute.” `SUPERVISION_FILTER` is a
union, and the title contains “enforcement,” so the item fails `AI_FILTER` and
still wins. “Information in the Age of AI” is lower in the same column. The
router test currently expects a regulation home story to beat practice. The
2026-09-20 lead is supervision-shaped and not an AI story.

### 8. The SBOM audit note does not cover the XML parser that reads untrusted feeds

`fast-xml-parser` is a devDependency. `.github/workflows/feed-audit.yml` gates
on `npm audit --omit=dev --audit-level=high`, which stays green while the high
advisory on that parser is invisible. `docs/SBOM.md` says the gate tracks the
React runtime and should not be weakened. That part is right, and it is not
the whole risk.

`scripts/fetch-feeds.ts` and `scripts/validate-feeds.ts` raise entity caps to
`maxEntityCount` / `maxTotalExpansions` 100000 and `maxExpandedLength` 1000000.
The comment justifying that cites GitHub release Atom and Simon Willison.
This source list has no `releases.atom` URL. The caps weaken the library’s
expansion limits on every RSS body CI downloads.

`decodeEntities` in `scripts/fetch-feeds.ts` calls `String.fromCodePoint` with
no range check. An out-of-range numeric entity throws out of `fetchOneFeed`.
That rejection is not caught per feed, so one bad item fails `fetchAllFeeds`
and the data build.

P1.3 in `docs/FUTURE_IMPROVEMENTS.md` is “upgrade the advisory.” It does not
say to put this parser on the audit gate or to put the caps back down.

### 9. CI will deploy a bad or stale tree, and the weekly audit will not notice topical junk

`.github/workflows/refresh.yml`:

- `workflow_dispatch` has no `github.ref == main` guard. A dispatch from
  another branch builds and deploys Pages.
- `concurrency.cancel-in-progress: true` on `refresh-deploy` cancels an
  in-flight hourly deploy when a newer run starts.
- The data step `git push`es before `npm run build` and deploy. A rejected
  push fails the job, so that run never deploys. Combined with finding 3, a
  zero-article fetch still deploys the previous site and exits green.

`.github/workflows/feed-audit.yml` runs `validate-feeds`, which scores liveness,
parse, and a 60-day freshness window. It does not apply `requireAny`. A Reuters
GN feed full of M&A wires is `OK` if the XML is fresh. Actionable feed failures
do fail the job (`process.exit(1)`), which is useful, and they still do not
describe the homepage bug above.

### 10. Tests miss the paths that actually shipped bad rows

`package.json` `"test"` covers unwrap, router, `requireAny`, and
`groupStories` only. Nothing asserts:

- every broad or Google News source either has `requireAny` or an AI-scoped URL
  that was re-probed
- `Reuters AI (GN)` / `Axios AI (GN)` are aggregators
- “RIAs”, “A.I.”, “GPT4”, and “xAI”
- title dedup keeps both source names, or at least prefers priority / press
- brief fallback skips a non-AI column lead and refuses a Claude brief with
  zero valid citations
- `check-data` behavior when the previous file is preserved

`scripts/fetch-feeds.ts` still contains the GitHub-release title synthesizer
(`cleanGitHubReleaseTitle` and related helpers). No configured URL contains
`releases.atom`, so it does not run. It is leftover from the sibling
aggregator, in the same way the entity-cap comment is. It is not an AISI or
Gray Swan scraper.

## Improvements not already in FUTURE_IMPROVEMENTS.md

These are the code changes worth doing later. They are not done in this review.

1. Add `requireAny: AI_FILTER` to `Reuters AI (GN)`, `Axios AI (GN)`, and
   `GN: wealthtech AI`. Re-check Finextra AI and American Banker AI the same
   way; drop channel items that fail the filter. Confirm with a rebuild that
   Industry no longer shows inflation, parcel M&A, or firearms deals, and that
   Wealthtech row 1 fails closed when the title has no AI token.
2. Stop discarding the duplicate at fetch time. Keep a source list on the
   surviving article (or dedup after routing). When titles match, keep the
   higher-priority non-aggregator row. Then measure trending again before
   changing the 72-hour window or the Jaccard threshold.
3. Make `build:data` exit non-zero when it refuses to replace
   `headlines.json`, or have `check-data` fail when `generatedAt` is older
   than the run. Keep the previous JSON on disk either way.
4. Teach `requireAny` the plural and punctuation forms that showed up as
   misses (`RIAs`, `A.I.`, `GPT4`, `xAI`), and require the AI token in the
   **title** for Google News items so a summary tail cannot promote a
   geopolitics headline.
5. Treat `(GN)` suffixes as aggregators in both `router.ts` and
   `groupStories.ts`. Point the existing test at `Reuters AI (GN)`.
6. Use one lead order in `src/App.tsx`, `generate-brief.ts`, and
   `RIA_LEAD_RANK`. Include compliance in the brief’s “has an RIA item” set.
   If Claude returns no allow-listed URLs, use the fallback brief.
7. Decide whether an enforcement-only SEC item may be the site lead. If the
   lead should mention AI, require an `AI_FILTER` hit inside
   `SUPERVISION_FILTER` before that item can win `leadUrl`. Update the router
   test in the same change.
8. In the weekly audit, run `npm audit --audit-level=high` **without**
   `--omit=dev` for `fast-xml-parser`, or move that package to `dependencies`.
   Lower the entity caps to what these feeds need. Catch
   `fromCodePoint` failures per item. Leave the React-runtime gate as it is.
9. In `refresh.yml`, deploy only from `main`, and do not let a failed data
   push skip an otherwise good Pages deploy of the commit that was checked
   out. Add a source-list test so a new GN URL without `requireAny` fails CI.
10. For null dates, use one age rule in `scripts/lib/score.ts` and
    `src/lib/timeAgo.ts`. Prefer dropping undated items (`>=` the hard window)
    so they cannot sit in the 21-day lanes and skip the 30-day gate.
